# Due Diligence Checklists

**Summary.** Operational, reusable checklists for the three phases of ABCF due
diligence: **initial screening**, **enhanced due diligence (EDD)** for elevated/
high-risk counterparties, and **ongoing monitoring**. These implement the
[risk matrix](abcf-risk-matrix.md) tiers and the
[third-party vetting lifecycle](../compliance/third-party-vetting-lifecycle.md).
Copy them into your workflow and adapt. Every item should have an owner, a date,
evidence, and a disposition.

> Screening lists, registers, and rules change constantly. Verify each source
> live at the time of screening; record the source and date checked.

## Phase 1 — Initial screening (all counterparties)

- [ ] **Identity confirmed** — legal name, registration number, jurisdiction,
      address verified against a primary register.
- [ ] **Ownership captured** — direct owners and, to the extent available,
      beneficial owners recorded.
- [ ] **Sanctions / restricted-party screen** — OFAC (SDN + consolidated, apply
      50% rule), BIS Entity List, UN/EU/UK lists as applicable; date-stamped.
- [ ] **PEP screen** — owners, directors, UBOs against PEP sources.
- [ ] **Adverse media** — corruption, fraud, sanctions, enforcement, debarment.
- [ ] **Debarment / exclusion** — multilateral development bank and government
      debarment lists.
- [ ] **Jurisdiction tier** — CPI band + sector history recorded (see
      [cpi-integration.md](cpi-integration.md)).
- [ ] **Transaction context** — value, role at the official interface, how the
      counterparty was introduced.
- [ ] **Red-flag pass** — screened against [red-flag-indicators.md](red-flag-indicators.md).
- [ ] **Risk rating assigned** — via [abcf-risk-matrix.md](abcf-risk-matrix.md);
      disposition (proceed / EDD / escalate / decline) recorded.

## Phase 2 — Enhanced due diligence (elevated & high risk)

Triggered when Phase 1 yields an Elevated/High rating, a strong red flag, or a
state/SOE-plus-intermediary transaction.

- [ ] **Beneficial ownership resolved to natural persons** — full chain,
      including secrecy-jurisdiction layers; unresolved UBO treated as a finding
      (see [beneficial-ownership.md](../institutional/beneficial-ownership.md)).
- [ ] **Source of wealth / funds** — for high-risk owners and PEP-linked parties.
- [ ] **Track record verified** — the counterparty/intermediary actually has the
      staff, premises, and history to perform the work.
- [ ] **Independent references** — trade and banking references verified.
- [ ] **Official-nexus check** — any connection between the counterparty and the
      officials/customers who influence the award.
- [ ] **Site visit / interview** — for the highest-risk intermediaries.
- [ ] **Commission/fee reasonableness** — proportionate to genuine services;
      structure (success-fee, third-party payments) scrutinised.
- [ ] **Litigation / enforcement history** — deeper search across jurisdictions.
- [ ] **Contractual controls drafted** — anti-corruption reps/warranties, audit
      rights, no-subcontracting, payment controls, termination triggers,
      certification/training obligations.
- [ ] **Approval at the right level** — compliance sign-off; board/senior
      management for High.
- [ ] **Decision documented** — evidence, residual risk, and rationale retained.

## Phase 3 — Ongoing monitoring

- [ ] **Periodic re-screening** — sanctions/PEP/adverse-media at a cadence set by
      risk tier (higher tier → more frequent).
- [ ] **Trigger-based reviews** — ownership change, new licence/permit, value
      increase, adverse media, key-personnel change, jurisdiction CPI decline.
- [ ] **Relationship review** — performance vs. contract; anomalies in payments,
      invoices, or deliverables.
- [ ] **Audit-right exercise** — for high-risk relationships, on a schedule or
      on cause.
- [ ] **Certification refresh** — annual anti-corruption certifications and
      training acknowledgements (see
      [board-management-certification.md](../compliance/board-management-certification.md)).
- [ ] **Re-rating** — update the risk matrix rating; escalate or exit on
      material change.
- [ ] **Recordkeeping** — monitoring log maintained (also satisfies FCPA
      books-and-records expectations).

## JV-specific checkpoints

For joint ventures and minority stakes (see
[`../case-studies/jv-governance-failures.md`](../case-studies/jv-governance-failures.md)):

- [ ] **Partner diligence** — full EDD on JV partners and their UBOs.
- [ ] **Affiliate/associated-person mapping** — who can bind the JV (FCPA agency;
      UK Bribery Act s.7).
- [ ] **Governance rights** — board seats, reserved matters, information rights,
      audit rights proportionate to the stake and risk.
- [ ] **Compliance obligations flowed down** — JV-level anti-corruption program,
      training, reporting, and certification.
- [ ] **Payment-flow visibility** — ability to see and test JV disbursements to
      third parties.
- [ ] **Exit / remediation levers** — rights to investigate, suspend, and exit on
      findings.
