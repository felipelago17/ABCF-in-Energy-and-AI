# Case Studies — Methodology & Misattribution Notes

**Summary.** These case studies read major enforcement matters as
**governance-network failures** — asking *which centre should have detected the
conduct, and why it didn't* — rather than merely restating penalties. Because
secondary reporting routinely garbles the *statute*, *amount type*, and *which
company* a matter belongs to, this file sets the sourcing rules and maintains a
running list of commonly-misattributed matters. **Verify every figure against
the linked primary source before relying on it.**

## Method

Each case study covers:
1. **The facts** — who, what, when, which authority, which statute, how much
   (with the *type* of amount stated), cited to a primary source.
2. **The governance-network reading** — where in the polycentric system
   detection/deterrence failed (see
   [`../institutional/polycentric-governance.md`](../institutional/polycentric-governance.md)).
3. **The transaction-cost angle** — what the risk/control economics were (see
   [`../institutional/transaction-cost-economics.md`](../institutional/transaction-cost-economics.md)).
4. **Remediation / lessons** — the controls that would have changed the outcome.

## Sourcing rules

- **Primary sources only for facts** — DOJ/SEC press releases and orders, SFO
  announcements, court filings, statute text. Secondary sources for context only.
- **State the amount *type*** — total, U.S. portion, criminal fine,
  disgorgement, forfeiture, or combined global resolution. These are constantly
  conflated.
- **Name the statute precisely** — FCPA anti-bribery vs. FCPA books-and-records/
  internal-controls vs. sanctions (IEEPA/OFAC) vs. export controls (BIS) vs. UK
  Bribery Act s.6/s.7. A large penalty against an oil-services or tech company is
  **not automatically an FCPA matter**.
- **Date precisely** — year of resolution (ISO or "Month YYYY").

## ⚠️ Commonly-misattributed matters

Help keep this list current via the
[case-submission / correction template](../.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/case-submission.md).

| Claim seen in circulation | Correction |
|---|---|
| "Shell 2016 ~$105M FCPA settlement" | **No such resolution is verifiable.** Shell's actual FCPA matter is the **2010 Panalpina-related settlement (~$48M total: $30M DOJ + ~$18.1M SEC)**. Treat the "2016/$105M" figure as unverified/false absent a primary DOJ or SEC document. |
| "Bonny Island LNG is a Shell FCPA case" | Bonny Island is the **TSKJ joint-venture** matter. **KBR/Halliburton** settled in **2009 for $579M** ($402M DOJ + $177M SEC). Shell was **not** a defendant. See [fcpa-energy.md](fcpa-energy.md). |
| "Shell was convicted/settled over OPL 245" | **No U.S. conviction or settlement.** DOJ closed its inquiry (~2020) without charging Shell; Shell, Eni, and executives were **acquitted** in the Italian criminal trial (**March 2021**). |
| "Schlumberger 2015 ~$232.7M FCPA settlement" | **Sanctions, not FCPA.** It was a **2015 DOJ guilty plea for conspiracy to violate U.S. sanctions on Iran and Sudan** (IEEPA) — ~$232.7M ($155.1M fine + $77.6M forfeiture). Not a bribery case. |
| "Weatherford 2016 FCPA settlement" | **2013**, ~**$253M**, and a **combined** resolution across **DOJ, SEC, OFAC, and BIS** — FCPA (internal-controls/anti-bribery/books-and-records) **plus** export-control/sanctions violations. See [fcpa-energy.md](fcpa-energy.md). |
| "ENRC was convicted of bribery" | **No criminal conviction.** The long-running SFO investigation produced none; the well-known case law — *SFO v ENRC* **[2018] EWCA Civ 2006** — is about **legal privilege**, and *ENRC v Dechert/Gerrard* **[2022] EWHC 1138 (Comm)** is civil litigation about the investigation's conduct. See [uk-bribery-prosecutions.md](uk-bribery-prosecutions.md). |
| "There is an AI-vendor FCPA case" | **Not as of 2026.** The closest technology precedents are **enterprise-software/IT vendors** (e.g., **SAP, 2024, ~$220M**). Do not imply a frontier-AI-specific FCPA resolution exists. See [fcpa-ai-technology.md](fcpa-ai-technology.md). |
